Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 17, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to TalkRush. Your privacy and digital security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our suite of free online web tools, such as the Image Resizer, Password Generator, and others.

2. Local Processing & Zero Uploads

Important: Our core tools are designed to operate entirely locally within your web browser. We respect your privacy by not touching your sensitive files.

  • Files & Images: When you use tools like the Image Resizer, your photos are never uploaded to our servers. All processing, resizing, and compressing happens directly on your device using client-side technologies.
  • Generated Data: When you use our Password Generator, your secure passwords are created locally. We do not track, log, save, or transmit the passwords you generate over the internet.

3. Information We Collect

Because our tools process your tasks locally, the information we collect is strictly limited to standard, non-identifiable web traffic metrics:

  • Usage Data: Basic analytics such as IP address (anonymized), browser type, referring pages, and device information to help us maintain and improve our website.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for basic site functionality, analytics, and serving advertisements.

4. Data Retention Policy

  • No File Storage: Since your files and generated data never leave your browser, we have absolutely no user files or data stored on our servers to retain or delete.
  • No User Accounts: We do not offer or require account registration. Therefore, we do not collect, store, or retain personal details like names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Automatic Log Expiration: Any temporary technical data (such as basic server traffic logs) is automatically rotated and purged by our hosting providers.

5. Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements, which allows us to keep our tools 100% free. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can control your ad preferences or opt out of personalized advertising by visiting: Google Ads Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use essential third-party services, such as website hosting and basic analytics, to keep TalkRush running smoothly. These third-party services process minimal technical data under their own respective privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Because our web tools perform their functions locally on your device, your files and generated data remain completely under your control. We utilize secure HTTPS encryption across our website to ensure that your connection to our platform is safe and secure.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we introduce new tools and features to TalkRush. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with the revised date mentioned at the top.
