How a chat works, start to finish

  1. Open text chat or webcam roulette and choose a nickname. You do not need to sign up.
  2. Add interest tags such as gaming, anime, movies or music, and pick who you want to meet if you like.
  3. Press start. TalkRush looks for someone online with matching tags first, then anyone available.
  4. Talk. Send text, voice messages, images or GIFs, or switch to video.
  5. Not feeling it? Press Esc on desktop or tap skip and you are with someone new in seconds. Enjoyed it? Add them as a friend and continue in your inbox.

Because there is no sign-up, there is no waiting queue and no profile to fill in. Most people are in a conversation within a few seconds of landing.

Chat safely

Anonymous does not mean risk free. A few habits keep conversations pleasant:

  • Never share bank details, passwords or one-time codes (OTPs), even with someone friendly.
  • Keep your full name, address, school or workplace to yourself until you truly know someone.
  • Use skip and the report button freely. You never owe a stranger a reply.
  • Turn the camera on only when you are comfortable, and be careful with photos you send.
  • Treat people as you want to be treated. Harassment and spam get people reported.

Questions people ask

Is TalkRush really free?

Yes. Text chat, video roulette, rooms and the inbox cost nothing, and there are no tokens or paid tiers to unlock them.

Do I need to register or install an app?

No. It runs in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on phone or computer. You choose a nickname and start. You can add it to your home screen if you want app-like access.

Can I choose who I talk to?

You can add interest tags and use gender filters. For a specific person, create a private room and send them the code, or set a password on a group room.

How do I skip someone?

Press Esc on desktop or tap skip on mobile. You are matched with another stranger straight away.

Are video calls private?

Video and audio use direct peer-to-peer connections between you and your partner. Still, treat any stranger carefully and never show or share anything you would not want to keep.

Can I send photos, GIFs and voice messages?

Yes, in 1v1 chats and in rooms. For temporary pictures, PhotoShare gives a 5-digit code and deletes the image after 24 hours.

Who can use it?

TalkRush is for people aged 18 and over.
