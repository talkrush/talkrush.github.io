About the Free Text Counter Tool

Our online Text Counter is a fast, secure, and privacy-focused tool designed for writers, students, and SEO professionals. Unlike other tools, your text is processed entirely in your web browser. No data is ever sent to our servers, ensuring your sensitive documents remain 100% private.

Why Track Character and Word Counts?

  • Social Media: Platforms have strict limits (e.g., Twitter: 280 characters, Instagram captions: 2,200 characters).
  • SEO Meta Tags: Keep your meta titles under 60 characters and descriptions under 160 characters for optimal search engine visibility.
  • Academic Writing: Easily meet minimum or maximum word count requirements for essays and assignments.

How is Reading Time Calculated?

The estimated reading time is based on the average adult reading speed of 225 words per minute. This feature is particularly useful for bloggers and speechwriters who need to estimate how long it will take an audience to consume their content.
