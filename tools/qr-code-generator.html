Tips for Best Results

When generating your code, keep in mind that the more text you enter, the denser and more complex the visual pattern becomes. Highly dense QR codes can be difficult for older smartphone cameras to scan, especially if printed in a small size. For the best scanning reliability, keep your URLs as short as possible. Additionally, always remember to test scan your downloaded QR code with your own phone before adding it to any printed materials.
